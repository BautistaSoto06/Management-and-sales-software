#Sales and Inventory Management System

##Description

This project consists of the development of a Sales and Inventory Management System, designed to help businesses efficiently manage products, control stock, and register sales operations.

The system is focused on small and medium-sized businesses, aiming to optimize processes and improve internal organization.

##Objectives
###Automate product and stock management
###Register sales quickly and accurately
###Improve inventory control
###Generate useful reports for decision-making
###Main Features
------------------------------------------------------------------------
##Product Management
###Create, update, and delete products
###Manage stock levels
###Update product prices
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
##Sales Management
###Register new sales
###Automatically calculate totals
###View sales history
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
##Reports
###Best-selling products
###Sales by period
###Revenue tracking
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
##User Management
###Authentication system (login)
###Role-based access (Admin / Employee)
